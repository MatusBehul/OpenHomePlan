# Family house — owner's brief

The brief this example house was designed from. Comments and `/apply-comments` should keep
the design within it unless the owner says otherwise.

## The house (owner's specifications)

- Total living area: **150–200 m²**, **2 floors**
- **Ground floor:** one big open **living room with kitchen**, a **bathroom** (or WC + shower)
- **Upper floor:** **3 bedrooms**, a **bathroom**; the **master bedroom has its own walk-in closet**
- **Garage** (optional, attached or detached, single-storey)
- **Terrace** (would be appreciated)
- **Swimming pool** (would be appreciated)
- **Greenhouse** in the garden (maybe)
- Not yet specified, so pick sensible defaults and note them as assumptions: plot size,
  orientation (assume street to the north, garden to the south), roof type, style.
